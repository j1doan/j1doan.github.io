James Doan

I am a master's candidate at the UC San Diego Halıcıoğlu School of Data Science and Computing, advised by Professor Tianhao Wang.

My research interests are generative models, efficient LLM inference, parallel computing, hardware-aware optimization, and applied statistical learning.

I received my bachelor's degree in Cognitive Science at UC San Diego. After graduating, I spent some time in neuroscience research at the Mobley Lab before pivoting into artifical intelligence research at UC San Diego.
